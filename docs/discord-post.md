⚔️ **OctoArenaFrames v1.0.7** – arena frames made for OctoWoW 🐙

Every enemy on your screen: every cast, every kick, every cooldown. Free, and **OctoKicks is built in**.

**🧩 The frames**
❤️ Health, power, class, spec and enemy number (1-5) in slots that never swap
🎯 Who each enemy targets – `> YOU` in red
🏅 PvP trinket + racial, bright when ready
📏 2v2 / 3v3 / 5v5 sized by itself, each with its own place and size

**⚡ Casts & kicks**
📊 Fast cast bars, coloured by school, grey when it can't be kicked
🛑 See the kick land, then `LOCKED: Frost 5.2` for the lockout
🟢 Your own kick beside their cast: green = kick it now
🖱️ Mouse over a frame + any offensive spell = it goes to that enemy, your target stays

**⏱️ Cooldowns**
📖 Read from **OctoWoW's own spell data**, not retail (Spell Lock 30s, WotF 150s...)
🧠 Learns talented cooldowns per player
🔄 Cold Snap, Preparation, Readiness and shared cooldowns (Pummel ↔ Shield Bash)

**😵 Crowd control**
🖼️ The worst CC on them shows on their class icon, with a timer
📉 DR: next one lands at 50% / 25% / IMM
🛡️ Gold edge while they're immune

**🔌 DLLs – all optional, it works without any**
✅ **SuperWoW** – exact tracking + health for every enemy *(recommended)*
✅ **nampower** – instant aura and spell events
✅ **ClassicAPI** – finds the enemy team by name, pushback, focus
➕ **UnitXP** – fades enemies out of range / line of sight
Get them in one click with the **Octo DLL Installer**: <https://github.com/bdot89/OctoDLLInstaller/releases/latest>

**⬇️ Install**
🔗 Launcher: add `https://github.com/bdot89/OctoArenaFrames.git` (it updates itself)
📦 Or the **OctoArenaFrames-1.0.7.zip** from <https://github.com/bdot89/OctoArenaFrames/releases/latest> (not "Source code")
